How to train it fast. Every print is recorded automatically. Prints that finish cleanly are marked good on their own. For failures, open the print, slide to the first frame where it went wrong, and tap Failure started here. You need 3 failed and 3 good prints to start; 10+ failures in different colors gets you a strong model. To get failures on purpose: print a tall thin tower and knock it over mid-print, or skip glue on PETG/ABS.
